Best Practices for Detection

  • Good Lighting: Keep your room well-lit. Shadows or high backlighting can degrade MediaPipe's hand boundary detection.
  • Hand Position: Keep your hand flat and centered in the webcam frame. Move it slightly closer or further to adjust sizing.
  • Sentence Building: Hold any sign shape steady for approximately 1.5 seconds (10 frames) to auto-speak and register it as a word.
  • Custom Recording: Record at least 20–30 frames from different angles when saving custom gestures to train high-quality GNN & MLP models.
